Radhe Radhe,

In this series i will be giving away my own personalized notes and guides for becoming a linux wizard from an administrative prespective. We will try to understand together that `how admins think, how they perform day to day crucial tasks inside CLI and how we can leverage those techniques to do our own small tasks`.

Plus we will be utilizing RedHat Linux which is also the industry standards for doing admin related tasks. keep in mind that just because it's an `admin POV`, doesn't mean that it cannot work in day to day tasks. But rather you will find yourself doing most of the tasks in your day to day to life if you are planning to use linux as your daily driver. 


**Another thing to keep in mind is that all of the tasks we will be doing on RedHat Linux, so a lot of commands may differ if you are using your own seperate distro or planning to use later on. You can find docs related to those specific distros on the internet pretty easily.**


> [!IMPORTANT]
> **There's no prerequisites to start learning from this series. I will be guiding you from installtion of RedHat Linux to performing crucial admin related tasks.**
